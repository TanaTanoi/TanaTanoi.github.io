New Steam Demo Update!

Welcome to our very first Shape Sender Deluxe newsletter! And what a way to kick it off - with the launch of an update to the demo!

In case you missed it, we have a Steam demo which has 16 levels PLUS a level editor - download and share levels with your friends and community. That's basically infinite content!

In this update, there's a whole range of bug fixes and performance improvements but the biggest change is Controller Support! Tubular! 

Sendy's Southern Hemisphere Tour!

Sendy's been traveling! First we hit NZGDC where some game developers got a sneak peek behind the curtain to see how the game is made. Then, at PAX AUS, hundreds of people came by the booth and said hi, so thanks for coming along! Then the last stop was at ACMI in Melbourne - this is the first time the game' ever been in a museum which was AMAZING!

This is how 102 different people played our PAX exclusive level! Players had to work around the previous player's solution (which sometimes created chaos). Can you spot yours?

Going Independent!? Delay? 

Dead Teapot is going full indie! We're no longer partnering with Dear Villagers to bring you the game and we're gonna do it ourselves 💪 It's an unexpected but exciting change but does have a few caveats. Firstly, we'll be delaying the launch until early 2027, and secondly, we'll only be releasing on Steam & Switch (for now 👀). 

It's definitely a little different but it's also all back in our hands now, so that's fun too.
